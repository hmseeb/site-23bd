Our Story

Nearly Two Centuries of Southern Hospitality

The house at 615 College Street began in 1839 as a simple two-room dwelling built by George Marsh. In 1869 it was bought by Colonel John Ashford, a North Carolina infantry officer, and his wife Elizabeth, who added two rooms downstairs and a full second floor above. The Ashford family held it for more than a century.

In 1995 it became an inn. Today, under McPherson Hospitality, it is still the same house — original heart pine floors, tall windows, elegant fireplaces, a hand-crafted library — but with fast Wi-Fi, private baths and easy self check-in. TripAdvisor ranks it the #1 inn in Clinton, and Facebook guests recommend it 100%.

"A refreshing alternative to standard hotels."

  • Walk everywhere. Steps from Sampson Regional Medical Center, the courthouse, and downtown shops and restaurants.
  • Easy to reach. One hour from Raleigh, one hour from Wilmington, twenty minutes from I-95.
  • Everything included. Free on-site parking, table linens, furniture, A/V and internet.
